Privacy Policy — Studio Tools

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What the tool accesses

With your permission, Studio Tools uses the YouTube API Services to access the YouTube channels you own or manage: channel and video analytics (read-only), video upload, playlists and scheduling, and comments (read, reply, hold for review).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show reports, upload videos and reply to comments for your own channels.
  • Access tokens are stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's key file on your computer removes all stored tokens.

Google and YouTube terms

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
